How to play
  1. On your turn, flip the top word card.
  2. Read the word out loud.
  3. Roll the die.
  4. Write the word that many times in the next spaces on the shared board.
  5. Team game: fill the board together. Versus game: the player who wrote the most words wins.

Use first names or nicknames only. Nothing is saved on a server. Players connect directly to the host's browser.
